# GigaSaudi.com — Decision Log

A record of strategic decisions affecting the positioning, language, and architecture of GigaSaudi.com.

## Decision 001

**Date:** 2026-06-29  
**Decision:** Reframe from "availability" language to "strategic review" language across all site pages and supporting documents.

### Reason

The prior language framing ("Strategic Availability," "Inquire," "Begin Qualified Inquiry") positioned the asset correctly in category but signaled a domain-sale transaction to a strategic buyer arriving without context. A buyer capable of acquiring this asset at its category value does not respond to domain-market language — they respond to the logic of reviewing a strategic asset.

The new framing shifts the buyer's first-10-second perception from "a beautiful domain" to "a strategic asset that requires internal review." The next step is not asking a price — it is reviewing a memo.

### Changes Applied

- **Nav label (all pages):** "Availability" → "Review" (URL path `/availability/` unchanged)
- **Home page hero-claim:** Single sentence replaced with 3-line governing statement: "The projects are named. / The scale layer is not. / GigaSaudi names that layer."
- **Home page:** Added "Why This Matters to a Strategic Owner" section with 4 owner-type cells
- **Home page CTA:** "Strategic Availability" → "Request Strategic Memo"
- **Availability page title:** "Strategic Availability" → "Strategic Review"
- **Availability page opening:** Replaced retail-adjacent language with reframed positioning statement
- **Availability page axiom:** "Available only as a qualified strategic acquisition" → "Available for qualified strategic review"
- **Availability page:** Added new Section 02 — "Why This Matters to a Strategic Owner" with 4 owner-type rows
- **Availability page sections:** Renumbered 02-06 → 03-07
- **Availability page CTA:** "Begin Qualified Inquiry" → "Request Strategic Memo"
- **Inner pages (sectors, sources, saudi-giga-scale, why-gigasaudi):** Nav label and doc-nav CTA updated consistently
- **New file:** `STRATEGIC_REVIEW_MEMO.md` — pre-dossier strategic brief for qualified acquirers

### Boundary (Unchanged)

The following constraints were preserved verbatim across all changes:

- No public price stated anywhere on the site
- No buy-now flow or retail acquisition pathway
- No marketplace language (no auction, no listing, no bid)
- No claim of official Saudi government affiliation, endorsement, or operational connection

### Next Phase

The strategic review framing creates the condition for a qualified acquirer to request the memo. If and when the `STRATEGIC_REVIEW_MEMO.md` is shared with a prospective acquirer, it functions as the pre-dossier brief — the document that moves the conversation from initial contact to structured review.

No further public-facing changes are required at this stage.

## Decision 003

**Date:** 2026-06-29  
**Decision:** Remove all public valuation references from GigaSaudi.com.

### Reason

Public price anchors limit strategic upside and can reduce a category-level asset to domain-market logic. GigaSaudi.com must be evaluated privately by qualified acquirers based on intended deployment, acquisition scope, and strategic relevance.

### Boundary

No public price, no valuation range, no entry threshold, no marketplace-style offer framing.

### Changes Applied

- **Home page:** Replaced public acquisition value tiers with non-price private review stages: Qualified Context, Strategic Memo Review, and Private Terms Review
- **Home page CTA note:** Replaced auction/listing transfer language with the private strategic acquisition terms boundary
- **Public dossier:** Reframed the public value section as Private Strategic Review
- **Printable dossier:** Replaced acquisition threshold tiers with private strategic review stages
